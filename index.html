A little history, freshly dusted

Rust and Rose, owned by Maggie Lane, is a shop where every piece has lived a life before finding its way here — a farmhouse dresser, a chippy-painted mirror, a stack of linens edged in lace. Maggie sources them one estate sale and one back-road antique fair at a time.

The shop is small, the welcome is warm, and there's always a fresh find waiting near the door. If country charm and a well-worn story are your kind of decorating, you'll feel right at home here.
